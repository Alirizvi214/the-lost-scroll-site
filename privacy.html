Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

The Lost Scroll is a YouTube channel about mythology and legends. It uses a small private application to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel.

What the application accesses

The application requests one Google permission, the YouTube upload scope. It is used only by the channel owner, and only to upload and schedule the channel's own videos.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about visitors, viewers or any other users. The application has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking. The only credential it stores is the channel owner's own authorization token, kept as a private secret and used solely to upload that owner's videos.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not transferred to others, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.
